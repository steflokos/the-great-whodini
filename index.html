Privacy and connection notes
  • Your chat and the game itself go straight from one browser to the other, encrypted. They never pass through the room server.
  • The free PeerJS server (0.peerjs.com) only helps the two of you find each other. It sees the room code and both IP addresses while you connect. Your opponent can see your IP address too, because that's how peer-to-peer works.
  • Colors, emoji and initials are drawn on your own device. Photos and GIFs you upload are shrunk where needed and sent straight to your opponent. If a character uses a picture from a web link, the person joining gets asked before it loads, since loading it shows their IP address to that site.
  • If it won't connect, a strict firewall or VPN is usually the reason. Try a different network, like a phone hotspot. Someone technical can also set up a TURN relay (see CUSTOM_ICE_SERVERS in the file).
